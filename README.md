its jus doom on the worst console ever

# Origin story
I was just sitting on my chair one day, doing absolutely nothing. Then i remembered that i used to have a psp i would turn to in these situations, until that was taken from me months ago. then i remembered, that years ago, i used to have one of those bootleg chinese psp knock-offs called the "PVP"... yeah as in dream vs technoblade. Anyways, i was thinking about all the games i used to play on it(mario, contra, spy vs spy, and this one weird tmnt fighting game), and just thought to myself- "Yo doom on this would be pretty cool". And now, here we are...
